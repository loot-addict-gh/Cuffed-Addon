**An addon for Cuffed that adds more restraints and more ways to apply them.**

> Requires \[Cuffed](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/cuffed) 1.3.15+

>
> Optionally supports \[Curios](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/curios) and \[Fake Players](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/fake-players)

## Features:

* New restraints: Rope, Straitjacket, Sleep Mask, and four blindfold-and-muffle combinations.
* Bed and Wall Restraints.
* A Player Picker that turns a restrained player into a carriable item.
* A remote controlled Shock Collar, on a restraint slot of its own.
* A Reinforced Bow that fires restraints and shocking arrows.
* Traps: dispensers that apply and remove restraints, redstone-fired pillories and wall restraints.
* Three restraint enchantments: Repellent, Restraint Gaze and Illusion.
* A compact restraint HUD, Curios-aware frisking, and three optional resource packs.
